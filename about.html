About

Sami Meszaros, Founder & Principal

Reno, Nevada — building the administrative infrastructure that lets growing organizations focus on their real work.

Origin

Meszaros Administrative Solutions was founded on a simple observation: many small organizations either have no administrative infrastructure at all, or are running on outdated, undocumented processes that no longer serve them — and often can't yet justify a full-time hire to fix it.

I started this practice to close that gap: to be the person who builds the systems before you're big enough to need a full department, and modernizes the ones you've outgrown.

Background

I bring more than seven years of operational leadership experience to every engagement, most recently building a Quality Management program from the ground up for a nationally accredited air medical transport organization (CAMTS).

That work means real, hands-on depth in compliance and policy infrastructure — not theoretical familiarity. It's the same rigor I bring to every project, whether it's a full administrative build-out or a single policy rewrite.

Mission

To build the administrative backbone growing organizations have never had.

And to modernize the systems that haven't kept pace — so leaders can focus on their core work instead of operational chaos.

Approach

Structured. Documented. Built for how you actually work.

From scratch, by design

Most administrative support providers step into systems that already exist. My methodology is built for organizations starting from zero — structured and documented from day one, so nothing depends on institutional memory.

Built to hand off

Every system I build is designed so your organization can eventually hire and onboard a full-time employee into a role that's already set up to succeed — or so you can keep me on as ongoing support. Either way, you're never locked in.
