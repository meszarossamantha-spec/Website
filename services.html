Services

Three ways to work together. One goal: a system that works.

These tiers describe how you engage with Meszaros Administrative Solutions, not fixed bundles of services. Every service area below can be delivered through any of the three tiers — whether you're a solo founder preparing for your first hire, or a team of up to 15 that's outgrown what you're managing from memory.

Tier 1

Build the Administrative Program

I design and build the infrastructure your organization needs — whether that means setting up structure before your first hire, or preparing an existing team to hand off to a future full-time employee. A curated program built from exactly what you need, not an automatic package of everything.

Typically priced as a flat fee, scoped from a free discovery audit.

Tier 2

Ongoing Admin Management

I run the administrative function(s) directly, for organizations that need consistent support but aren't ready for a full-time hire.

Typically an ongoing monthly or retainer-style engagement.

Tier 3

Project-Based Support

Targeted help with one specific, defined pain point, scoped to exactly what you need and nothing more.

Typically billed hourly or as a prepaid hour block.

How It Starts

Every engagement begins with a free Discovery Audit.

A 60–90 minute session, capped in both time and scope to one client-identified pain point. I review existing documents and systems first, then interview stakeholders — producing an internal findings summary and a client-facing proposal.

Discover

Learn how the organization actually runs, through document review, observation, and interviews, including identifying leadership's top pain point.

Build

Design and build the system, policy, or process the organization needs, tailored to how the team actually works.

Handoff or Stay On

Take the finished system and run with it, or retain Meszaros Administrative Solutions as ongoing support.

Pricing

Pricing depends on what your organization needs.

Flat-fee builds, retainers, and hourly work are all priced around the specific scope we define together — there's no one-size-fits-all rate. Every engagement is quoted after a free Discovery Audit, once I understand exactly what you need.

How payment works

Engagements begin upon signed contract and deposit. Flat-fee projects are 50% deposit with the remainder split across milestones; hour blocks split 50/50; hourly engagements start with a deposit and are invoiced at defined project checkpoints.
